## Section 1 — Unit Conversions

!!! info "Key conversions for this section"
    | Equality | To convert |
    |---|---|
    | 1 kg = 1000 g | kg→g: multiply by 1000 |
    | 1 g = 1000 mg | g→mg: multiply by 1000 |
    | 1 mg = 1000 mcg | mcg→mg: divide by 1000 |


---

!!! example "Problem 1"
    Convert 0.045 kg to g.

??? question "Hint — Metric prefix ladder"
    | Prefix | Symbol | Relative to base unit |
    |---|---|---|
    | **kilo** | k | × 1,000 |
    | *(base)* | g, L, m | × 1 |
    | **milli** | m | ÷ 1,000 |
    | **micro** | mc *(nursing)*, μ | ÷ 1,000,000 |

    To convert: count the steps between prefixes, then
    multiply or divide by the appropriate factor of 1,000.

??? success "Answer"
    \[0.045 \cancel{\text{ kg}} \times \frac{1000 \text{ g}}{1 \cancel{\text{ kg}}} = 45 \text{ g}\]

??? tip "Walkthrough"
    The conversion factor is 1 kg = 1000 g. Orient it with
    **kg in the denominator** so it cancels with the starting
    quantity, leaving g:

    \[0.045 \cancel{\text{ kg}} \times \frac{1000 \text{ g}}{1 \cancel{\text{ kg}}} = 45 \text{ g}\]

    **Quick mental check:** *kilo* means ×1000, so kg→g
    shifts the decimal three places to the right.
    0.045 → 45. ✓

!!! example "Problem 3"
    Convert 750 mcg to mg.

??? question "Hint — Direction check"
    mcg is a *smaller* unit than mg. Converting from a
    smaller unit to a larger unit always gives a smaller
    number. If your answer is larger than 750, the fraction
    is flipped.

??? success "Answer"
    \[750 \cancel{\text{ mcg}} \times \frac{1 \text{ mg}}{1000 \cancel{\text{ mcg}}} = 0.75 \text{ mg}\]

??? tip "Walkthrough"
    The conversion factor is 1 mg = 1000 mcg. Orient it with
    **mcg in the denominator** so it cancels:

    \[750 \cancel{\text{ mcg}} \times \frac{1 \text{ mg}}{1000 \cancel{\text{ mcg}}} = 0.75 \text{ mg}\]

    **Quick mental check:** 1000 mcg = exactly 1 mg.
    750 mcg is three-quarters of 1000, so the answer is 0.75 mg. ✓

## Section 2 — Oral Tablets

!!! example "Problem 4"
    **Order:** metoprolol 100 mg orally
    **Stock:** 50 mg per tablet
    **How many tablets?**

??? success "Answer"
    \[100 \cancel{\text{ mg}} \times \frac{1 \text{ tablet}}{50 \cancel{\text{ mg}}} = 2 \text{ tablets}\]

---

!!! example "Problem 5"
    **Order:** warfarin 7.5 mg orally
    **Stock:** 5 mg per tablet
    **How many tablets?**

??? success "Answer"
    \[7.5 \cancel{\text{ mg}} \times \frac{1 \text{ tablet}}{5 \cancel{\text{ mg}}} = 1.5 \text{ tablets}\]

    Verify the tablet is scored before splitting.

    !!! danger "High alert medication"
        Warfarin is a high alert medication. Verify the dose
        and stock concentration with an independent second
        check before administering.

## Section 4 — Weight-Based Dosing (Adult)

!!! example "Problem 10"
    **Order:** gentamicin 5 mg/kg IV
    **Patient weight:** 80 kg
    **Stock:** 40 mg/mL
    **How many mL?**

??? success "Answer"
    \[80 \cancel{\text{ kg}} \times \frac{5 \cancel{\text{ mg}}}{1 \cancel{\text{ kg}}} \times \frac{1 \text{ mL}}{40 \cancel{\text{ mg}}} = 10 \text{ mL}\]

---

!!! example "Problem 11"
    **Order:** vancomycin 15 mg/kg IV
    **Patient weight:** 176 lb
    **Stock:** 500 mg/10 mL
    **How many mL?**

??? success "Answer"
    Convert weight:
    \[176 \cancel{\text{ lb}} \times \frac{1 \text{ kg}}{2.2 \cancel{\text{ lb}}} = 80 \text{ kg}\]

    Full chain:
    \[80 \cancel{\text{ kg}} \times \frac{15 \cancel{\text{ mg}}}{1 \cancel{\text{ kg}}} \times \frac{10 \text{ mL}}{500 \cancel{\text{ mg}}} = 24 \text{ mL}\]

---

!!! example "Problem 12"
    **Order:** ibuprofen 10 mg/kg orally, max 600 mg
    **Patient weight:** 85 kg
    **Stock:** 200 mg/5 mL
    **How many mL?**

??? success "Answer"
    Calculated dose:
    \[85 \cancel{\text{ kg}} \times \frac{10 \text{ mg}}{1 \cancel{\text{ kg}}} = 850 \text{ mg}\]

    850 mg exceeds max of 600 mg — **use 600 mg**

    Volume:
    \[600 \cancel{\text{ mg}} \times \frac{5 \text{ mL}}{200 \cancel{\text{ mg}}} = 15 \text{ mL}\]

---

!!! example "Problem 13"
    **Order:** morphine 0.1 mg/kg IV PRN
    **Patient weight:** 154 lb
    **Stock:** 10 mg/mL

??? success "Answer"

    \[70 \cancel{\text{ kg}} \times \frac{0.1 \cancel{\text{ mg}}}{1 \cancel{\text{ kg}}} \times \frac{1 \text{ mL}}{10 \cancel{\text{ mg}}} = 0.7 \text{ mL}\]

??? success "Answer"

    \[250 \cancel{\text{ mg}} \times \frac{5 \text{ mL}}{125 \cancel{\text{ mg}}} = 10 \text{ mL}\]

## Section 6 — IV Flow Rates & Infusion Time

!!! example "Problem 17"
    Order: 1000 mL 0.9% NaCl over 8 hours.
    What is the flow rate in mL/hr?

??? success "Answer"
    \[\frac{1000 \text{ mL}}{8 \text{ hr}} = 125 \text{ mL/hr}\]

---

!!! example "Problem 18"
    Order: 250 mL over 45 minutes.
    What is the flow rate in mL/hr?

??? success "Answer"
    Convert time:
    \[45 \cancel{\text{ min}} \times \frac{1 \text{ hr}}{60 \cancel{\text{ min}}} = 0.75 \text{ hr}\]

    Rate:
    \[\frac{250 \text{ mL}}{0.75 \text{ hr}} = 333.3 \text{ mL/hr}\]

    Round to: **333 mL/hr**

---

!!! example "Problem 19"
    A 1000 mL bag is running at 150 mL/hr.
    After 3 hours, how much fluid remains and how much
    longer will it run?

??? success "Answer"
    Volume infused:
    \[150 \text{ mL/hr} \times 3 \text{ hr} = 450 \text{ mL}\]

    Volume remaining:
    \[1000 \text{ mL} - 450 \text{ mL} = 550 \text{ mL}\]

    Time remaining:
    \[\frac{550 \text{ mL}}{150 \text{ mL/hr}} = 3.67 \text{ hr}\]

    Convert decimal to minutes:
    \[0.67 \text{ hr} \times \frac{60 \text{ min}}{1 \text{ hr}} = 40 \text{ min}\]

    **550 mL remaining — 3 hours and 40 minutes.**

## Section 7 — Drip Rates

!!! example "Problem 20"
    **Order:** 1000 mL over 8 hours
    **Tubing:** 20 gtt/mL
    **What is the drip rate in gtt/min?**

??? success "Answer"
    Convert time:
    \[8 \cancel{\text{ hr}} \times \frac{60 \text{ min}}{1 \cancel{\text{ hr}}} = 480 \text{ min}\]

    Calculate:
    \[\frac{1000 \text{ mL}}{480 \text{ min}} \times 20 \text{ gtt/mL} = 41.7 \text{ gtt/min}\]

    Round to: **42 gtt/min**

## Section 8 — IV Medications

!!! example "Problem 22"
    **Order:** vancomycin 1000 mg in 250 mL over 90 minutes
    **What is the flow rate in mL/hr?**

??? success "Answer"
    Convert time:
    \[90 \cancel{\text{ min}} \times \frac{1 \text{ hr}}{60 \cancel{\text{ min}}} = 1.5 \text{ hr}\]

    Rate:
    \[\frac{250 \text{ mL}}{1.5 \text{ hr}} = 166.7 \text{ mL/hr}\]

    Round to: **167 mL/hr**

---

!!! example "Problem 23"
    **Order:** heparin 1000 units/hr IV
    **Stock:** 25,000 units in 500 mL 0.9% NaCl
    **What is the flow rate in mL/hr?**

??? success "Answer"
    Concentration:
    \[\frac{25{,}000 \text{ units}}{500 \text{ mL}} = 50 \text{ units/mL}\]

    Flow rate:
    \[1000 \cancel{\text{ units/hr}} \times \frac{1 \text{ mL}}{50 \cancel{\text{ units}}} = 20 \text{ mL/hr}\]

---

!!! example "Problem 24"
    **Order:** dopamine 5 mcg/kg/min IV
    **Patient weight:** 60 kg
    **Stock:** dopamine 400 mg in 250 mL D5W
    **What is the flow rate in mL/hr?**

??? success "Answer"
    Dose per minute:
    \[60 \cancel{\text{ kg}} \times \frac{5 \text{ mcg}}{1 \cancel{\text{ kg}} \cdot \text{min}} = 300 \text{ mcg/min}\]

    Convert to mg/hr:
    \[300 \cancel{\text{ mcg/min}} \times \frac{1 \cancel{\text{ mg}}}{1000 \cancel{\text{ mcg}}} \times \frac{60 \text{ min}}{1 \text{ hr}} = 18 \text{ mg/hr}\]

    Concentration:
    \[\frac{400 \text{ mg}}{250 \text{ mL}} = 1.6 \text{ mg/mL}\]

    Flow rate:
    \[18 \cancel{\text{ mg/hr}} \times \frac{1 \text{ mL}}{1.6 \cancel{\text{ mg}}} = 11.25 \text{ mL/hr}\]

    Round to: **11 mL/hr**

---

!!! example "Problem 25"
    An IV is running at 25 mL/hr.
    **Stock:** morphine 50 mg in 500 mL 0.9% NaCl
    **What dose is the patient receiving per hour?**

??? success "Answer"
    Concentration:
    $$\frac{50 \text{ mg}}{500 \text{ mL}} = 0.1 \text{ mg/mL}$$

    Dose rate:
    $$25 \text{ mL/hr} \times 0.1 \text{ mg/mL} = 2.5 \text{ mg/hr}$$
